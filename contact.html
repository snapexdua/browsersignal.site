We do not operate a live technical support service or promise a response time.

For a factual correction

Include the affected BrowserSignal URL, what you think is inaccurate, the browser or operating-system version if relevant, and a reliable public source. We aim to distinguish outdated menu labels from incorrect technical claims. We do not accept payment to change editorial conclusions.

For a broken tool

Tell us which page, button and device type failed. If possible, describe the steps needed to reproduce it. Do not send screenshots containing passwords, authentication codes, account tokens, workplace domains, private browsing history or identity documents. Our tools do not transmit your troubleshooting answers to the site operator.

Not a vendor support channel

BrowserSignal is independent of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge vendors. For account restoration, fraud, managed devices and security incidents, use official support or your organization’s IT channel. BrowserSignal cannot take remote control of a device or inspect its browser configuration.
