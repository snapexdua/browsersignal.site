Distinguish permission from an active stream

The camera button requests video only and offers a local preview. The microphone button requests audio only. Opening one releases the previous stream. This page does not record, upload, play microphone audio or measure its volume.

Decide what to check next

If permission is denied, inspect this origin’s settings and the OS permission. A missing-device or busy-device response calls for checking the chosen input and other apps. When this page opens the camera but a meeting cannot, compare the meeting’s own origin and device choice. Press Stop when finished.
