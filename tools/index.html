Why small experiments work better

Browser problems have multiple plausible causes. Changing cookies, extensions, network settings, and browser preferences simultaneously may hide which factor mattered. A simple investigation starts with scope: is the failure one website, one profile, all websites, or the entire device? The tools are intended to organize those observations, not make invisible scans of your computer.

We do not connect to your browser’s private history, access your accounts, or run remote tests. You supply the symptoms. The suggestions are educational; follow your browser publisher’s official help when a setting or menu changes.

Privacy-first design

Tool answers stay in the webpage. The Fix Log saves drafts locally in your browser if you use that feature. Exported logs are generated on your device. Opening this page does not send your troubleshooting answers to BrowserSignal.
