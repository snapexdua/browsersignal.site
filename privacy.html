Privacy policy

How BrowserSignal handles browser-local tools and third-party advertising services.

Effective date: 9 November 2026. This policy describes the simple static BrowserSignal website and its optional browser-local tools. It is not a promise that third-party advertising providers collect no data.

Information you choose to send

If you email us at snapexdua@gmail.com, the email and information you include will be received through your email provider and used to answer or handle your message. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, session tokens, payment data or other secrets. No account-registration form is provided on this site.

Issue Pathfinder and Fix Log

The Pathfinder processes selections entirely in the page and does not send those answers to BrowserSignal’s servers. The Fix Log uses browser localStorage when you click Save, and can export a text file on your device. Deleting site data in your browser can remove saved log entries. You can clear the log with the tool’s Clear button. We do not operate a cloud sync service for these tools.

Advertising and third parties

Pages containing editorial articles may load Google AdSense scripts. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention or related purposes in accordance with applicable terms and law. Ads may not appear before a website is approved or when no inventory is available. Refer to Google advertising and privacy information ↗. Where legally required, the operator must configure an appropriate consent management solution before relevant ad services run.

Hosting and logs

The site is intended to be hosted on Vercel or a comparable static host. The hosting provider may process standard technical request information, including IP address and device-related details, for delivery, security and operations. BrowserSignal does not claim that hosting creates zero logs. See the hosting provider’s current privacy documentation for more information.

External links and updates

Official source links open third-party websites under those providers’ privacy policies. We may update this policy when site features, hosting or ad arrangements change. Contact snapexdua@gmail.com for privacy questions or correction requests.
