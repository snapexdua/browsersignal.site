Privacy policy

How BrowserSignal handles browser-local tools and third-party advertising services.

Updated 27 November 2026. This policy explains what happens when you read a page, choose a browser check, save a Fix Log or contact the publisher.

Information you choose to send

If you email us at snapexdua@gmail.com, the email and information you include will be received through your email provider and used to answer or handle your message. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, session tokens, payment data or other secrets. No account-registration form is provided on this site.

Issue Pathfinder and Fix Log

The Pathfinder processes selections entirely in the page and does not send those answers to BrowserSignal’s servers. The Fix Log uses browser localStorage when you click Save, and can export a text file on your device. Deleting site data in your browser can remove saved log entries. You can clear the log with the tool’s Clear button. We do not operate a cloud sync service for these tools.

Browser Lab diagnostics

Tools inspect browser-exposed capabilities only after a user starts a check. Temporary storage test keys are removed; camera and microphone streams remain local and are stopped by the user or when leaving the page. Reports are generated locally. Connection checks make ordinary same-origin requests to a static endpoint; hosting logs may record request metadata. No diagnostic results are uploaded by the lab code.

Advertising and third parties

BrowserSignal uses Google AdSense on its homepage and editorial articles. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this website or other websites. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to personalize ads and process information such as IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where a regional consent message is shown, use its controls to choose or revisit your options.

You can manage or turn off personalized advertising in Google My Ad Center and manage participating vendors’ choices at YourAdChoices. Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Hosting and logs

The production site uses a hosting provider to deliver its files. Page loads, connection checks and the three fixed workbench requests can generate ordinary request logs, including IP address, requested path, time and device-related headers. The DNS worksheet operates locally with numeric inputs. The workbench reads only its own sample responses and does not ask for an address to inspect.

External links and updates

Official source links open third-party websites under those providers’ privacy policies. We may update this policy when site features, hosting or ad arrangements change. Contact snapexdua@gmail.com for privacy questions or correction requests.
