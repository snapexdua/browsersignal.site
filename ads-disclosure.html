How BrowserSignal treats advertising, editorial independence and affiliate relationships.

BrowserSignal is built as a useful browser-support publication. It may display advertising through Google AdSense when eligible; there is no guarantee that ads will appear on every visit.

Editorial content versus advertising

We do not present paid ads as repair steps. BrowserSignal guides are written to make sense without advertisements. Our recommendations favor vendor documentation and lower-risk troubleshooting; the presence of an advertiser does not imply that a browser vendor endorses our articles.

Paid relationships

At launch, this version contains no sponsored articles, affiliate product placements or paid software recommendations. If those arrangements change, material relationships should be disclosed near relevant content and on this page.
